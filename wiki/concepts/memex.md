# Memex

Proposed by [[entities/vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]] in 1945: a personal device for storing books, records, and communications, with **associative trails** between items — private, curated, where connections matter as much as documents.

## Relevance

[[concepts/llm-wiki|LLM Wiki]] is Memex-adjacent in spirit: personal, curated, trail-rich. Bush could not solve *who maintains the trails*. The LLM takes that role — updating cross-refs, summaries, and contradictions without boredom.
