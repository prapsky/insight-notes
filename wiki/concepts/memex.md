# Memex

[[entities/vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]] proposed the Memex in 1945. It was a personal device for storing books, records, and messages. It would also create **associative trails**, or meaningful paths, between related items. The collection would be private and carefully organized. Connections would matter as much as the documents themselves.

## Relevance

[[concepts/llm-wiki|LLM Wiki]] is similar to the Memex in spirit. It is personal, carefully organized, and rich in paths between ideas. Bush could not solve the question of *who maintains those paths*. The LLM takes that role. It updates cross-references, summaries, and contradictions without getting bored.
