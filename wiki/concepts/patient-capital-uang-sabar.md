# Patient Capital (*Uang Sabar*)

Thesis ([[entities/indrawan-nugroho|Indrawan Nugroho]], [[sources/012-endgame-65-indrawan-nugroho|Endgame #65]]): long-horizon innovation (Amazon-style reinvestment, climate-scale bets, deep builds) needs **patient money** — capital that need not be recycled in a few years. Indonesia’s binding problem is often **impatient capital** layered on thin system liquidity, not only a shortage of ambitious founders.

## Structural contrast (as argued)

| | Patient (*uang sabar*) | Impatient |
|--|------------------------|-----------|
| Archetype | US pension / long-duration pools (~20–30y) | Local recycle ~7y; tengkulak-style months |
| Founder behavior | Reinvest; delay exit; play long indicators | Omzet harian; forced early exit |
| Policy pep talk | Insufficient alone | “Think long-term” while shackled to short recycle |

Amazon’s innovation + customer-centricity + cost loop, in his framing, was enabled by CEOs with power *and* by liquidity that didn’t demand fast return of principal.

## Thin M/GDP bridge

He cites circulating money / GDP ~**45%** (Indonesia) vs advanced ~**150–250%** and Singapore ~**125%**. Raising the ratio *with patient character* → room for unicorns outside the usual centers. Same order-of-magnitude scarcity story as [[concepts/money-supply-vs-interest-rates]] (Purbaya M0 growth; Chatib savings/ICOR ceiling; Gita/Martyn elite-narrative scarcity) — different prescription emphasis (patient *duration* and founder culture, not only stock of M).

## Bootstrap when capital won’t wait

Anecdote: Indian MSME-digital firm refused large VC with a **7-year exit** clause; bootstrapped; delayed salaries until a revenue threshold; stayed independent. Not a universal model — a proof that mission + patience can substitute when local VC clocks conflict with the product’s clock.

## Founder patience = capital patience

Demographics may flood the world with capital chasing finite traditional assets — but if *operators* still optimize daily omzet or quick crypto bucks, patient pools don’t land. Educate long-game UMKM/startup behavior (active users, deferred personal cuan) alongside investor duration. Complements [[concepts/deep-tech-long-termism]] (Martyn: decade IP builds; money ≠ panacea) without requiring deep-tech specifically.
