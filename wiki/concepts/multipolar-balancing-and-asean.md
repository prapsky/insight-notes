# Multipolar Balancing and ASEAN

Claim ([[entities/chatib-basri|Chatib Basri]] with [[entities/gita-wirjawan|Gita]], [[sources/006-endgame-216-chatib-basri|Endgame #216]]): Indonesia’s workable posture is **escaping the binary** US vs China choice — until forced (e.g. incompatible IoT stacks) — by playing multiple clubs and thickening **ASEAN as a single entity**.

## Instruments named

| Instrument | Role in their framing |
|------------|----------------------|
| **BRICS** participation | Balancing / avoid G7-dominated club capturing all gains |
| **OECD** track | Parallel engagement (risk of “which side?” questions) |
| **RCEP** | ASEAN-as-bloc vs China/US; more “developing economy pulse” than TPP-era framing Gita rejected as too far ahead of domestic sellability |
| **Trilateral** US–China–ID | Track 1 / 1.5 on digital, energy transition, critical minerals (nickel majority Chinese-owned → US market-access bind) |

China = technology source; G7/US = capital source (M2 scale). China’s dual-circulation + BRI under-delivery vs pledges (SEA illustration) limits capital export speed — Indonesia must still bargain with location, minerals, market size.

## Soft power as hard constraint

Economic-security / friend-shoring era: **foreign policy leads**, economics supports (advanced-economy pattern). Needs soft power and storytelling capacity — India omnidirectional diplomacy as model; Singapore ambassadors get called to narrate SEA because they deliver *and* tell. Train Indonesians to **ask sharp questions** (AI-era pedagogy) as soft-power substrate — see [[concepts/stem-talent-pipeline]].

## Bilateralization risk

As multilateralism frays, bilateral deals favor the strong (Gita’s “tailor 10 coats / you make 5 — take my factory” image). ASEAN collective bargaining was partly invented to avoid that trap. Chatib: developing countries are structurally disadvantaged in pure bilaterals — hence RCEP/ASEAN logic still matters even if plurilateral/bilateral tactics rise.

## Bravo 500 panel (#007): tech stack optionality

Same Gita thesis at AI infra altitude: SEA (~700M people, ~$4T GDP) must keep **optionality** — not be cornered into US *or* China. [[entities/sai-prasad|Sai]] (Google): coexistence with Chinese players is workable; pick best available tech; AI is ~**10%** of the problem (engine vs airline — brand, ops, safety, customers are local). Complements [[concepts/copy-with-pride-application-layer]] and [[concepts/physical-vs-cryptographic-sovereignty]].
