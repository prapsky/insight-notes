# Deep-Tech Long-Termism

Thesis ([[entities/martyn-terpilowski|Martyn Terpilowski]], [[sources/005-endgame-272-martyn-terpilowski|Endgame #272]]): Indonesia’s last tech cycle rewarded **short-horizon consumer/TAM plays** and connection-led fundraising; durable growth needs **exportable IP**, engineering-heavy products, and ~10-year generational builds — with capital subordinated to management quality and due diligence.

## Short-termism pathology (as described)

- Copy US consumer apps; TAM / “280M people” as thesis; little IP that travels
- Circular VC: follow bigger names; weak data rooms; paid revenue; friends/family deals
- Sell shares on the way up; founders/VCs exit to wealth or government while companies lose money
- Tickets too large for the opportunity set → forced allocation into few “unicorns”
- Government startup bets with scarce fiscal capacity (his skepticism)

## Long-termism markers

| Marker | Contrast |
|--------|----------|
| Deep tech / B2B product | MSME apps, financial-inclusion theater, AI-agent me-too |
| Engineering core | Marketing-budget “tech” |
| Regional export plan | Indonesia-only scale behind connections |
| Own capital / skin in game | Raise forever; fail upward into adviser/VC seats |
| Decade horizon | Get-rich-quick; McKinsey → founder in months |

[[entities/bhumi-varta-technology|Bhumi Varta Technology]] is filed as his working counterexample (Vietnam/Japan push; distributor conversion). Vietnam peers: FPT, TMA, VinFast — returnee + FDI-sponge path.

## Money is not the panacea

Aligns with — but sharpens — vault themes on capital:

- [[concepts/money-supply-vs-interest-rates|Money growth]] can widen who can fund ideas (Gita’s M/GDP bridge), yet misallocated boom capital still destroys value
- [[concepts/fdi-climate|FDI]] into real capability (chips, EV, deep tech partnerships) > protecting middlemen
- [[concepts/risk-as-innovation-fuel|Risk as fuel]] needs *honest* failure, not recycled prestige after blowing up companies

## Pivot hope

Tech “bloodshed” may force short- → long-termism — *if* elites accept noses out of joint when self-made outliers and foreign sponges arrive ([[concepts/meritocracy-vs-patronage]]).
