# Investment Debottlenecking

Removing **field-level barriers** to investment that persist even when regulations look clean on paper. Central binding constraint in [[entities/purbaya-yudi-sadewa|Purbaya]]’s path from ~6.5% to ~8% growth.

## Paper vs field

New rules proliferate to “fix” each failure mode; on paper, obstacles vanish. In the field, complaints, rent-seeking, and non-implementation remain. Domestic actors can also **refuse** foreign projects even when terms improve (naphtha cracker anecdote).

## Satgas model (2016–19 experience he cites)

- Weekly case hearings; filter which complaints to try
- Order of magnitude: hundreds heard, ~193 resolved, investment impact cited ~Rp830T
- Chair: Yasonna Laoly; Purbaya as deputy
- Intended reboot under Prabowo: multi-pokja satgas (priority spending / private bottlenecks / regulatory cleanup)

## Enforcement lever

Finance ministry as more than “juru bayar”: withhold or delay transfers if ministries don’t execute — a **credible threat**, plus regular reporting to the president. Past meetings without threat → promised rule changes that never landed.
