# Platform Leadership and Ecosystem Expansion

[[entities/indrawan-nugroho|Indrawan]], citing Annabelle **Gawer** and Michael **Cusumano**, argues that the most powerful technology companies are not always those that sell the most units. They are often the companies that become the **center of an ecosystem**.

Developers build on their platforms. Companies depend on them. Users spend more of their time inside them. As participation grows, the platform becomes more useful and harder to leave. This is called a network effect. Source: [[sources/011-indrawan-nvidia-ai-pc|#011]].

## Nvidia example

- Nvidia is **already a platform leader in data-center AI** through **CUDA**, with about a decade of developer lock-in.
- **RTX Spark and AI PCs** extend that platform from data centers into personal computers *before* competitors control local or “edge” computing.
- This is not only a new chip. It expands Nvidia’s **role** in deciding how AI is used, rather than merely increasing GPU sales.

## Why harder than Apple Silicon

Apple controlled its chips, operating system, hardware, and major applications. Nvidia must coordinate with Microsoft, many computer manufacturers, and developers.

Industry **habits and compatibility** often matter more than raw technical specifications. Trust, adoption, and clear benefits for users matter more than adding “AI” to the brand.

## Strategy posture

Take a position while the **AI PC category is still unclear**. Waiting for certainty can mean that the best positions are already taken. Success in today’s data-center market can become a **blind spot** that hides the next area of competition.

## Vault bridges

- This is the platform version of [[concepts/value-creation-vs-value-capture]] and [[concepts/ai-commoditization-and-enterprise-lock-in]]. Own the layer that customers find difficult to leave as models become widely available.
- AI on personal devices complements Bravo’s point about distributed and agent-based computing, as well as [[concepts/ai-electrification-threshold]]. Electricity remains a constraint even when computing moves to a different location.
- “Act before certainty” is the company-level version of [[concepts/uncertainty-to-risk-translation]].
