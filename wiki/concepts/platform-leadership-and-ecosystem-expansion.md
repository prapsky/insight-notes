# Platform Leadership and Ecosystem Expansion

Claim ([[entities/indrawan-nugroho|Indrawan]], [[sources/011-indrawan-nvidia-ai-pc|#011]], citing Annabelle **Gawer** & Michael **Cusumano**): the most powerful tech firms aren’t those selling the most units but those that become the **center of an ecosystem** — developers build on them, companies depend, users live inside; network effects raise exit costs.

## Nvidia instance (his telling)

- **Already platform leader in DC AI** via **CUDA** (~decade of developer lock-in)
- **RTX Spark / AI PC** = expand that platform from data-center AI into personal computers *before* rivals own the edge
- Not just a new chip: **role expansion** (who defines how AI is used) vs selling more GPUs

## Why harder than Apple Silicon

Apple controlled chip + OS + hardware + key apps. Nvidia must coordinate Microsoft, many OEMs, and developers; industry **habit and compatibility** often beat raw specs. Trust/adoption and clear user benefits beat “AI” branding.

## Strategy posture

Take position while the **AI PC category is still vague** — waiting for certainty means best seats are gone. Today’s DC success can be a **blind spot** that hides the next front.
