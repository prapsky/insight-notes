# Weaponized Interdependence and Substitutes

Claim ([[entities/keyu-jin|Keyu Jin]], [[sources/009-endgame-239-keyu-jin|Endgame #239]]): the more entangled economies become, the more **leverage** one party can use — and the faster that leverage **unravels**, because modern substitutes appear quickly. Short-term pain for the targeted does not equal long-term victory for the hegemon.

## Instances she cites

| Leverage | Induced response |
|----------|------------------|
| US dollar / SWIFT / Fed primacy | Urgency for alternative payment systems and currencies |
| China rare-earth export controls | Incentive to build alternatives and find substitutes |
| Historical: Napoleon continental system; British vs Germany/US; US vs Japan | Suppression fails in the long run |

## Related claims in the same interview

- US “victim” framing ignores services-export dominance and dollar privilege
- Tariffs/bifurcation are **inflationary** for Global South via Chinese intermediate goods (~30% manufacturing share claimed) — even “rerouted” Mexico/Canada/EU goods often embody Chinese inputs
- Hegemony never permanent; knowledge/AI diffusion shortens catch-up cycles
- “Jungle rules” diplomacy damages credibility path-dependently (allies don’t reset on the next president)
