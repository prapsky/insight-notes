# Journalism Fracture

Claim ([[entities/jiang-xueqin|Jiang Xueqin]], [[sources/008-endgame-259-jiang-xueqin|Endgame #259]]): post-WWII print/TV journalism once supplied a shared national narrative; that privilege is dead. After ~2016 the profession (in his telling) abandoned fair/balanced pretenses, aligned with security narratives, and lost trust — audiences splinter into bubbles; corporate response is **AI worldview moderation**.

## Arc he narrates

1. **Golden age ideal** — Gay Talese apprenticeship: truth-seeking, underdog lens, working-class outsider critique
2. **2016 break** — “TDS”; Russiagate/security-apparatus prominence; then COVID maximalism; Ukraine jingoism
3. **Exit to independents** — Jimmy Dore, Tucker Carlson named as truth-seeking alternatives in his personal path
4. **Inflection** — Corporate consolidation/anxiety pushes people away; AI assistants threaten to replace reading/thinking; antidote = individual responsibility to seek, debate, admit error + open/transparent AI

Complements [[concepts/idea-contest-and-centrism]] (Tom): substance media as culture change — but Jiang’s tone is darker (capture and Matrix risk), Tom’s more constructive (podcasts as soft path to centrism). Same object: who narrates reality.
