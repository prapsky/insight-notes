# Government 4.0 and Agile Regulation

Claim ([[entities/chatib-basri|Chatib Basri]], [[sources/006-endgame-216-chatib-basri|Endgame #216]]): developing countries should worry less about catching **Industry 4.0** than about **Government 4.0** — the state writing rules that are obsolete within months while statutes cannot change at tech speed.

## Diagnosis

- Postcard → WhatsApp; USB → AirDrop: regulators still drafting for the last medium
- Detailed rules (how to implement) age instantly; laws are hard to amend
- “Agile bureaucracy is an **oxymoron**” — don’t pretend the machine can sprint like a startup

## Prescription: principles over prescriptions

Shift mindset from **agree on rules** to **agree on principles**:

- Example: financial inclusion + no fraud + consumer protection — leave methods to the market/regulator judgment
- Don’t legislate USB-era mechanics into permanent law

Pairs with private-sector **reskilling** (double tax deduction for firm training) because government training centers (BLK) often lag tools — industrial policy via incentives, not obsolete public workshops.

## Link to AI / capital

AI is framed as **capital-intensive engineering** (scale, data centers), not “just software.” Supremacy concentrates in US/China megafirms; watching from the sidelines without joining capital/talent networks worsens [[concepts/middle-class-squeeze|elitization]]. Regulation that can’t keep up amplifies that lag.
