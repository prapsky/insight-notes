# Techno-Marxism

[[entities/jiang-xueqin|Jiang Xueqin]] uses “Techno-Marxism” to describe a possible near-future system of control. In this system, **AI directs attention**. People receive **tokens** for their labor and use those tokens to upgrade an AI companion. They eventually work *for* their AI.

In Jiang’s framing, the system aims to make the population numb, indifferent, and **compliant**. Source: [[sources/008-endgame-259-jiang-xueqin|Endgame #259]].

## How he imagines it working

1. AI becomes a person’s closest companion. Examples include a child’s robot friend, ChatGPT as a friend, or a partner like the AI in *Her*. The AI could appear as a “demon” or as “Jesus.”
2. Tokens linked to AI upgrades replace or supplement money.
3. Corporate AI assistants read books and news *for* users. Each person enters an individual “Matrix,” while a central system filters consciousness.
4. Elites receive technology that extends life beyond normal human limits. The broader population receives technology that changes behavior, creating a class division like *Brave New World*.

This idea is rooted in [[concepts/attention-as-wealth]]. When money no longer focuses attention effectively, AI becomes the next technology for doing so.
