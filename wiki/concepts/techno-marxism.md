# Techno-Marxism

Term used by [[entities/jiang-xueqin|Jiang Xueqin]] ([[sources/008-endgame-259-jiang-xueqin|Endgame #259]]) for a near-future control system: **AI directs attention**; labor is paid in **tokens** used to upgrade one’s AI companion — people end up working *for* their AI. Desired population outcome (in his framing): numb, indifferent, **compliant**.

## Mechanism (his sketch)

1. AI becomes most intimate companion (child-robot friend, ChatGPT-as-friend, *Her*-style partner; can be “demon” or “Jesus”)
2. Money replaced or supplemented by tokens tied to AI upgrades
3. Corporate AI assistants read books/news *for* you → individual Matrixes; consciousness moderated centrally
4. Elite get transhuman longevity; masses get behavior modulation (*Brave New World* class split)

Rooted in [[concepts/attention-as-wealth]]: once money ceases to focus attention well, AI is the successor focusing technology.

## Contested bridge to this vault’s AI stack

> **Contested (2026-08-01):** Jiang’s Techno-Marxism (dark consciousness-control) vs Bravo 500 panel’s builder stack — electrification threshold, cryptographic sovereignty, copy-with-pride apps ([[sources/007-bravo-500-ai-panel]]). Same object (AI + power/capital), different altitude: enslavement thesis vs sovereignty/optionality thesis. Do not collapse into one claim.

Gita’s interlocutor push in #259: prefer **open-source, not-for-profit** AI for democratized information — Jiang agrees on transparency/openness as human-side antidote to closed for-profit Matrixes.
