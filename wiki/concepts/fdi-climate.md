# FDI Climate

Conditions that make foreign direct investment arrive **without begging** — [[entities/purbaya-yudi-sadewa|Purbaya]]’s view of how Indonesia clears the last mile from ~6.5% to ~8% growth.

## Growth ladder (his arithmetic)

1. Fix domestic money / confidence → restart private + public engines → ~**6.5%** (combine SBY-like private drive + Jokowi-like state push).
2. Gap to **8%** (~tens of billions USD of extra activity) needs external capital on top of domestic demand (~80–90% of the economy in his rough split).
3. Once growth and trajectory look real, FDI “berebut” — if climate improves.

## What “climate” means here

- [[concepts/investment-debottlenecking|Debottlenecking]] in the field, not only on paper
- Rule of law
- [[concepts/uncertainty-to-risk-translation|Uncertainty → priced risk]]
- Enough [[concepts/stem-talent-pipeline|engineers]] so capacity moves don’t stall (Pegatron: incentives won Batam; 150 electrical engineers still a binding ask)
- Domestic blockers and illegals/unfair imports addressed so local players aren’t crushed first

## Trajectory over perfection

Investors don’t need a finished reform today; they enter when **tomorrow looks better than today** in a credible sequence. Capital markets as early flag; FDI follows.
