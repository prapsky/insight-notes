# Elite Overproduction

Frame Jiang attributes to **Peter Turchin** ([[sources/008-endgame-259-jiang-xueqin|Endgame #259]]): elites always rule; the instability comes when **too many elite aspirants** compete for limited ruling slots — a zero-sum fight to impose one’s reality.

## US application (Jiang’s cut)

| Bloc | Base | Ambition (his words) |
|------|------|----------------------|
| **Transnational capital** | Wall Street, City of London, Dubai, Hong Kong | Continuity of dollar/finance order (Clinton/Obama era peak) |
| **Technological elite** | Silicon Valley (Nvidia, OpenAI, Microsoft named) | Replace dollar system with **AI system**; expect state bailout if bubble bursts (2008 parallel) |

Same abundance narrative as **dot-com 2000** (“you don’t get it, unlimited growth”) now retold as AI utopia — mostly unprofitable circular financing in his view.
