# Elite Overproduction

Jiang attributes this idea to **Peter Turchin**. Elites always rule, but instability grows when **too many people want to join the elite** and too few ruling positions are available. They enter a zero-sum struggle in which one person’s gain is another person’s loss. Each group tries to impose its view of reality. Source: [[sources/008-endgame-259-jiang-xueqin|Endgame #259]].

## Jiang’s application to the United States

| Bloc | Base | Ambition (his words) |
|------|------|----------------------|
| **Transnational capital** | Wall Street, City of London, Dubai, Hong Kong | Continue the dollar-based financial order, which peaked in the Clinton and Obama eras |
| **Technological elite** | Silicon Valley, including Nvidia, OpenAI, and Microsoft | Replace the dollar system with an **AI system**; expect a government bailout if the bubble bursts, as in 2008 |

Jiang sees the same story of unlimited abundance that surrounded the **dot-com bubble in 2000**: “You don’t get it; growth is unlimited.” He says the story has returned as an AI utopia. In his view, much of its financing moves in circles and does not produce profits.

## Bridge to other vault pages
