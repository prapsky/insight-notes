# Brain Gain and Circulation

This page covers national strategies for developing talent, as presented by [[entities/gita-wirjawan|Gita]] and answered by [[entities/martyn-terpilowski|Martyn]] in [[sources/005-endgame-272-martyn-terpilowski|Endgame #272]]. The [[concepts/stem-talent-pipeline|STEM pipeline]] page focuses on the number of skilled people produced at home. This page focuses on **movement and openness**: who enters, who returns, and who teaches local people.

## Four illustrations (Gita)

| Model | Exemplar | Idea |
|-------|----------|------|
| Brain **train** | Japan | Build excellent talent at home. In his framing, about 80% of Japan’s Nobel path never went overseas. |
| Brain **gain** | Australia | Open the country to global talent and use the arriving expertise to build a top-tier economy. |
| Brain **circulation** | China | Send millions abroad for STEM education. Many return and help industrialize the country. |
| Brain **linkage** | India | A large diaspora remains abroad but continuously exchanges ideas with people at home. |

Gita estimates Indonesia’s diaspora at about 200,000–300,000 people over 50 years. This is small compared with more than 7 million from India and more than 9 million from China. Indonesia lags in both scale and openness.

## Martyn’s addenda

1. **Work abroad, not only study abroad** — Returning immediately after an MBA may show that family connections work at home but not abroad. The most valuable returnees may have 20 years of operating experience.
2. **Be a sponge** — Vietnam hosted Intel’s fully foreign-owned investment in 2005 and later built firms such as FPT, TMA, and VinFast. Japan first used foreign finance experts and later built Japanese-run funds. China hosted foreign firms, copied what worked, and then competed globally. Local-content requirements that block real supply chains do not provide useful protection.
3. **Meritocracy is the welcome mat** — Top talent won’t enter markets where achievement isn’t recognized ([[concepts/meritocracy-vs-patronage]]).
4. **Accessible STEM, not only elite MBAs** — Fancy schools without scholarships reproduce the same surnames; Vietnam–EU scholarship pipelines as talent-pool builders.

## Link to FDI

Here, a good [[concepts/fdi-climate|FDI climate]] lets foreign firms create jobs and paths to higher skills *before* the country insists on local champions. Absorb knowledge first and copy later. It also makes visas easier for scarce senior experts. In the SpaceX-engineer thought experiment, one expert would need more foreign colleagues and confidence in the rule of law.

## Chatib (#216): diaspora work + split faculty

- Graduates should be allowed to **work abroad** so the diaspora becomes larger and denser. Chatib describes the goal as having more people like Gita at Stanford and Harvard who can serve as informal ambassadors.
- Universities can offer split appointments that let members of the diaspora teach in Indonesia for part of each year.
- Campuses should recruit the best teachers in each subject, including foreigners. Chatib notes that the Faculty of Economics at the University of Indonesia historically tended to hire its own alumni first.
- Storytelling can become the international public face of talent circulation and a source of soft power. India and Singapore serve as narrators of Asia in this framing. See [[concepts/multipolar-balancing-and-asean]].

## Bravo 500 panel (#007): playground + pay + steered scholarships

Gita repeats the four models. [[entities/budi-gunadi-sadikin|Budi]] says **STEM without a place to use it** leads Singapore to absorb Indonesian talent. Scholarships should target the digital economy and three hubs: the San Francisco Bay Area, Beijing and Tsinghua, and Bangalore. This is better than an open menu of MBA programs. [[entities/franky-widjaja|Franky]] says firms should retain people through **higher pay** and import mentors, because people are the only asset. These ideas complement Martyn’s sponge model and Chatib’s preparation and faculty proposals, while AI infrastructure remains scarce ([[concepts/ai-electrification-threshold]]).
