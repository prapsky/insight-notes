# Brain Gain and Circulation

National strategies for cultivating talent, as framed by [[entities/gita-wirjawan|Gita]] and answered by [[entities/martyn-terpilowski|Martyn]] in [[sources/005-endgame-272-martyn-terpilowski|Endgame #272]]. Complements the domestic [[concepts/stem-talent-pipeline|STEM pipeline]] page (supply/scale) with **mobility and openness** (who enters, returns, and teaches locals).

## Four illustrations (Gita)

| Model | Exemplar | Idea |
|-------|----------|------|
| Brain **train** | Japan | Build indigenous excellence at home (~80% Nobel path never overseas, per his framing) |
| Brain **gain** | Australia | Open gates; import global talent → top-tier economy |
| Brain **circulation** | China | Send millions abroad for STEM; many return; industrialize |
| Brain **linkage** | India | Large diaspora stays abroad but exchanges ideas with home continuously |

Indonesia’s diaspora (~2–300k over 50 years in Gita’s figure) is small vs India (~7M+) / China (~9M+); scale + openness both lag.

## Martyn’s addenda

1. **Work abroad, don’t only study abroad** — MBA-then-home often means connections work here; names don’t abroad. Desired returnees = 20-year operators.
2. **Be a sponge** — Vietnam (Intel 100% FDI 2005 → FPT/TMA/VinFast), Japan finance (expats then Japanese-run funds), China (host → copy → compete globally). Local-content theater that blocks supply chains isn’t protection.
3. **Meritocracy is the welcome mat** — Top talent won’t enter markets where achievement isn’t recognized ([[concepts/meritocracy-vs-patronage]]).
4. **Accessible STEM, not only elite MBAs** — Fancy schools without scholarships reproduce the same surnames; Vietnam–EU scholarship pipelines as talent-pool builders.

## Link to FDI

[[concepts/fdi-climate|FDI climate]] here means letting foreign firms create jobs and skill ladders *before* insisting on local champions — sponge first, copy later — plus easier visas for scarce senior skills (SpaceX-engineer thought experiment: needs follow-on foreign talent + rule-of-law confidence).
