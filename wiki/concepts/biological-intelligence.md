# Biological Intelligence

Frame from [[entities/michael-levitt|Michael Levitt]] ([[sources/004-endgame-193-michael-levitt|Endgame #193]]): three intelligences on Earth — **Human (HI)**, **Artificial (AI)**, and **Biological (BI)**. BI is the deep one: humans did not invent it; it is “alien” intelligence we can learn from.

## Core claims (attributed)

- BI invented molecules, self-assembly, and evolution.
- A protein is a ~5 nm machine that **assembles itself**.
- Human manufacture is **outside-in** (factory); biology builds **inside-out**.
- AI neural nets inherit a 1943 nerve-cell model — AI is already biologically informed.
- Practical lesson for civilization: treat BI’s strategies (especially diversity) as design priors — see [[concepts/diversity-as-evolutionary-strategy]].

## Why it matters in this vault

Bridges science ingest to AI enthusiasm: Levitt treats LLMs as tools continuous with earlier computational biology / ML, not a rupture into pure silicon intelligence. Complements (does not replace) knowledge-system pages like [[concepts/llm-wiki]] and [[concepts/rag]].
