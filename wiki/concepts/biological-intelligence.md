# Biological Intelligence

[[entities/michael-levitt|Michael Levitt]] describes three kinds of intelligence on Earth: **human intelligence (HI)**, **artificial intelligence (AI)**, and **biological intelligence (BI)**. He calls BI the deepest kind. Humans did not invent it. It is an “alien” intelligence that we can study and learn from. Source: [[sources/004-endgame-193-michael-levitt|Endgame #193]].

## Core claims

These claims are Levitt’s:

- BI created molecules, self-assembly, and evolution.
- A protein is a machine about 5 nanometers wide. It **assembles itself**.
- Human factories usually build things **from the outside in**. Biology builds **from the inside out**.
- AI neural networks developed from a 1943 model of a nerve cell. AI has therefore been influenced by biology from the start.
- Civilization can use BI’s strategies as starting principles for design. Diversity is especially important. See [[concepts/diversity-as-evolutionary-strategy]].

## Why it matters in this vault

This idea connects science with interest in AI. Levitt treats large language models as another step in the history of computational biology and machine learning. He does not treat them as a complete break into purely silicon intelligence.
