# Ownership Society

Policy/culture idea [[entities/tom-lembong|Tom Lembong]] associates with the Anies campaign orbit ([[sources/003-endgame-248-tom-lembong|Endgame #248]]): build a **society of asset owners** so welfare comes from rising asset values (wealth effect), and align officials’ personal long-term wealth with **good policy** rather than short-term rent from permits.

## The licensing trap

Permit density is a symptom of three inputs: **money**, **entrepreneurial culture**, and **bureaucracy**. Where corruption is embedded, officials have no incentive to shorten chains — each recommendation/exception is sellable. Grey rules enable later accusation/threat as income.

## Two proposed breaks

1. **Party finance** — Fund much of party operating cost from APBN so parties need not park people in ministries chiefly to monetize policy/permits. Not a magic bullet; a start. (Tom: controversial but hard to avoid.)
2. **Long-term stakes** — Mechanisms so officials hold assets that appreciate under clear rules, cheap permits, and honest apparatus — NPV of clean growth beats weekly extraction. Requires leadership imagination to picture that equilibrium.

## Wealth effect

In advanced economies (Wall Street lens), prosperity is less wage/bonus than portfolio and property appreciation under stable growth — shared interest in a healthy climate.

## Link to FDI / debottlenecking

Same climate problem as [[concepts/investment-debottlenecking|debottlenecking]] and [[concepts/fdi-climate|FDI climate]], but emphasis is **incentive redesign** (parties + personal stakes), not only satgas case clearance.
