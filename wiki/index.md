## Concepts

### Knowledge systems

- [[concepts/llm-wiki|LLM Wiki]] — A lasting markdown knowledge base that an LLM maintains and improves over time
- [[concepts/rag|RAG]] — A method that finds raw documents before answering but does not save understanding between questions
- [[concepts/memex|Memex]] — Bush’s idea for a private, organized information store connected by related paths
- [[concepts/ingest|Ingest]] — The process of reading a raw source, adding its knowledge to wiki pages, and updating the index and log
- [[concepts/query-filing|Query Filing]] — The process of answering from the wiki and saving useful conclusions as pages
- [[concepts/wiki-lint|Wiki Lint]] — A health check for contradictions, unlinked pages, outdated claims, and missing knowledge
- [[concepts/schema|Schema]] — The `.cursorrules` conventions that guide the LLM while it maintains the wiki
