## Summary

Gita frames Indonesia’s AI ambition against an **energy and electrification gap**, then walks three lenses: **ex-policymaker / cabinet** (Budi — Jensen Huang stack, power wheeling, principle-based regulation, steered LPDP), **hyperscaler operator** (Sai — physical vs cryptographic sovereignty, agentic/distributed compute, sandboxes, SEA optionality), and **conglomerate builder** (Franky — power + data-center participation, copy-with-pride application layer, people as sole asset). Hub concepts: [[concepts/ai-electrification-threshold]], [[concepts/physical-vs-cryptographic-sovereignty]], [[concepts/copy-with-pride-application-layer]].

## Core claims (attributed)

### Backdrop — energy as AI precondition (Gita)

1. **Capacity gap** — China ~4,000 GW generation; US ~1,400; Indonesia ~100. Per-capita electrification: China/US ~10,000 kWh; SEA only Singapore & Brunei at that level; Indonesia ~1,300 kWh/capita.
2. **AI-capable ≈ 10,000 kWh/capita** — Gita’s camp: nations need ~that electrification intensity to be AI *creators*, not only consumers/enablers. LLM queries use ~10–50× energy of a Google search (his framing).
3. **Arithmetic of catch-up** — Getting ID to ~10,000 kWh/capita implies ~**600 GW** (~7× current). Build rate ~5 GW/yr → layman’s ~**120 years**. Daily data ~50,000 TB; ~200M cyber hacks/day (figures as stated — unverified here).
4. Bridges [[sources/006-endgame-216-chatib-basri|Chatib #216]] energy-transition financing realism (similar ~1,300 kWh and century-scale build math). See [[concepts/ai-electrification-threshold]].

### Jensen stack & power wheeling (Budi)

5. **Five layers (Jensen Huang framing)** — (1) energy, (2) chips, (3) infrastructure/DC+GPU, (4) models, (5) applications. Concern concentrated in 1–3; talent adequate for 4–5.
6. **Power wheeling blocked** — Proposed limited electricity-law deregulation (not privatization): allow hydro (green) generators to supply DC customers via leased PLN grid capacity, bypassing bureaucracy/technical bottlenecks. Gov + parliament rejected. Grid still coal-heavy while DC demand wants green power.
7. **Chip access** — ~90% advanced supply via TSMC; DC/GPU builds need direct or partnered Taiwan access.
8. **Lead times lengthening** — 1 GW plant: RFS ~**7 years** now vs ~4 years a decade ago. RUPTL 10-year plan exists, but small-scale DC/GPU capacity today ≠ hyperscale path.
9. **Principle-based regulation** — Competitive sectors (e.g. smart handsets) need principles not detailed rules; monopolies can stay rule-based. Aligns with [[concepts/government-4-0-and-agile-regulation]]. Mark Cuban frame applied to **countries**: AI countries vs former countries.
10. **Cabinet AI literacy hope** — Optimistic after late-2024 cabinet induction (facilitator named “Michael Houseman” in ASR — identity uncertain); wants light-touch, private–gov cooperation.

### Sovereignty under constraint (Sai)

11. **Physical vs cryptographic sovereignty** — Physical = local DC, local models/services. Cryptographic = trace outbound bytes; lock rules for which data is used for what. Policy often waits to “see” local presence; cryptographic control is the near-term path while global power/chip/silicon are constrained. See [[concepts/physical-vs-cryptographic-sovereignty]].
12. **Mid-level talent / productionization** — Economy knows AI as science projects; gap is mainstreaming — mid-tier operators who standardize within regulatory boundaries and use *existing* infra better.
13. **Agentic AI + distributed compute** — Agentic (perceive→process→plan→execute, iterative) uses disproportionately more energy than generative chat. Future = fit-for-purpose: cloud, laptop, or mobile (e.g. remote clinic with low bandwidth). Flip: start from outcome, work backward — not wait for global DC power sufficiency. Mythos/old-vuln anecdote: visibility tools can surface 27-year issues that sat “in front of our face.”
14. **Exponential tech vs linear regulators** — Singapore-style **gov AI sandboxes**; Google digital-literacy work with MoE and ministries. Ideation→**production** gap across FS/healthcare/retail. Start with today’s tech; never wait for simultaneous tech+reg readiness. Hello-world then guardrails (developer metaphor). Hack dwell-time anecdote: best detect ~30 min → ~15 sec.
15. **MedGemma / open models + MoH** — Example of available stack for primary-care triage so patients need not travel Papua/Sulawesi/Kalimantan to one specialist hub.

### Conglomerate stack & copy-with-pride (Franky)

16. **Sinarmas digital infra** — Participates across power (coal, geothermal, solar), land, and data centers (~**40+ MW** taken at time of talk; market talking 100–200 MW). Aims to ease government burden on generation.
17. **No Indonesian DeepSeek soon** — Timing late; talent + government support + capital-to-burn required; chances “not big.” Prefer **platform + localize layers** over reinventing foundation models. See [[concepts/copy-with-pride-application-layer]].
18. **Copy with pride** — Adopt Chinese education/healthcare/industrial platforms (accuracy claims ~95–97% in some healthcare subjects vs average doctor — **interview claim**); modify for Indonesian needs. Application layer is where Indonesia can compete; China may be restricted on some tech speed but leads on apps/scale/attitude/gov support.
19. **People as only asset** — Retain ~250k STEM grads; import mentors; pay more so talent doesn’t leave for Singapore. Transformation discomfort + choosing robust/safe solutions = top challenges. Wisdom stays with humans; AI takes repetitive work (NOC 500→10 anecdote from China visit).
20. **Sovereign data ≠ sovereign LLM** — Need sovereign *control* of data; many ways without building another DeepSeek.

### Talent, education, geopolitics (cross-panel)

21. **STEM without playground** (Budi) — Even 250k STEM grads leave if no field to deliver (Singapore absorbs). Conglomerates with full AI puzzle (power→DC→ops) are rare; need policy “playgrounds,” not only job slogans. Cross-link [[concepts/stem-talent-pipeline]].
22. **Four brain models** (Gita reprise) — Train (Japan) / gain (Australia) / circulation (China ~9M+ sent) / linkage (India ~7M+ diaspora). Manage talent like a portfolio. See [[concepts/brain-gain-and-circulation]].
23. **Steered LPDP / three Bays** (Budi) — When in government: scholarships only toward digital economy; destinations SF Bay, Beijing/Tsinghua, Bangalore — not open MBA menu. National LPDP should focus brands needed in 10–20 years.
24. **SEA optionality** (Gita + Sai) — ~700M people, ~$4T GDP; shouldn’t be forced US *or* China. Sai: pick best available tech; AI is ~**10%** of the problem (engine vs airline metaphor — brand, ops, customers, safety are local). Coexistence with Chinese players plausible; don’t over-focus bifurcation. Reinforces [[concepts/multipolar-balancing-and-asean]].
25. **Education** — Budi: Singapore preschool coding buses = 10–20y horizon; Indonesia can’t depend on government alone — private (e.g. Sinarmas education institutions) must align curriculum to 10–20y needs. Sai: personalized learning era; institutions supply knowledge/intent, AI extends creativity outside school. Gita: ~88% household heads without university (Sabang–Merauke); teachers/role models inject ambition, imagination, practiced serendipity. Franky: private sector educate + **pay** to keep talent. Soft-infra bridge: [[concepts/soft-infrastructure-teachers]].

## Notable quotes / framings

- Gita on modernity vs build rate: multiply capacity ~7× while adding only ~5 GW/yr — “120 years… unless we’re taking stem cell injections.”
- Budi / Mark Cuban → nations: companies (and countries) with AI vs *former* companies/countries.
- Sai on sovereignty: stop waiting for the “big bang” of local hyperscale; take cryptographic control and evolve.
- Sai on geopolitics: “I wouldn’t be too hung up on which engine I want to fit into my plane.”
- Franky on foundation models: “I don’t want to be another DeepSeek, and I don’t think I can.”
- Closing chorus: maximize AI *now* (Budi); never a better time than today (Sai); sit on the mega-trend + retain talent (Franky).
