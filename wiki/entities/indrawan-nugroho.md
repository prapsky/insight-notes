# Indrawan Nugroho

Indonesian innovation adviser and YouTube commentator — Founder & CEO of **Cipta Innovation / CIAS** (ASR variants: Jazz/Cipta). Appears in this vault in two modes:

1. **[[entities/endgame-podcast|Endgame]] #65** — long-form interview on storytelling, innovation culture, and patient capital
2. **YouTube strategy essays** — shorter frameworks on AI industry / platform moves (credited as Dr. Indrawan Nugroho)

## Formation (from #65)

- Lineage Solo / early Jogja; raised Jakarta. Grandfather named him toward “Indonesia Raya”; Indrawan Nugroho framed as anugerah of a son for Indonesia’s glory — mission identity he says would make a long green-card life feel like betrayal
- Melbourne commerce (wanted film school; crisis diverted); S2 UI psychology (industrial/organizational); S3 strategic management
- Theater, short fiction, video; COVID lockdown → YouTube as “connecting the dots” of that stack
- Advises startups and incumbents on innovation programs; pandemic remote delivery expanded client load
