# Indrawan Nugroho

Indrawan Nugroho is an Indonesian innovation adviser and YouTube commentator. He is the founder and CEO of **Cipta Innovation / CIAS**. Automatic speech recognition produced name variants such as Jazz and Cipta.

He appears in this wiki in two ways:

1. **[[entities/endgame-podcast|Endgame]] #65** is a long interview about storytelling, innovation culture, and patient capital. Patient capital is funding that can wait many years for results.
2. **YouTube strategy essays** present shorter frameworks about AI companies and technology platforms. These videos credit him as Dr. Indrawan Nugroho.

## Background (from #65)

Indrawan's family comes from Solo, and he spent his early years in Jogja before being raised in Jakarta. His grandfather chose his name with “Indonesia Raya” in mind. Indrawan explains the name as a gift of a son for Indonesia's glory. This gave him a strong sense of mission. He says that spending a long time in the United States on a green card would have felt like a betrayal of that mission.

He studied commerce in Melbourne, although he had wanted to attend film school. A crisis changed his path. He later completed a master's degree in industrial and organizational psychology at the University of Indonesia, followed by a doctorate in strategic management.

His experience includes theater, short fiction, and video. During the COVID lockdown, he began using YouTube to connect those skills and interests. He advises both startups and established companies on innovation programs. Remote work during the pandemic allowed him to serve more clients.
