# Sinarmas

Sinarmas, also known as the Sinar Mas group, is an Indonesian conglomerate. A conglomerate is a large group of companies working in different industries.

In the [[sources/007-bravo-500-ai-panel|Bravo 500 AI panel]], [[entities/franky-widjaja|Franky O. Widjaja]] says the group participates in coal, geothermal, and solar power generation. He also mentions land, telecommunications infrastructure, agribusiness, educational institutions, and data centers. At the time of the talk, he cites more than 40 megawatts of data-center capacity. He presents these parts as an almost complete “AI puzzle” compared with other companies.

## Why it appears here

Sinarmas is a specific example of private companies building AI infrastructure while Indonesia faces limited energy supply. The approach is to build power and data-center capacity, use foundation models developed elsewhere through [[concepts/copy-with-pride-application-layer]], and focus on keeping skilled people. It avoids building a national large language model only for prestige.

This example connects to [[concepts/ai-electrification-threshold]] and [[concepts/stem-talent-pipeline]].
