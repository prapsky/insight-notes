# Prabowo Subianto

Prabowo Subianto is the President of Indonesia in the context of [[sources/002-endgame-245-purbaya-sadewa|Endgame #245]]. In that interview, [[entities/purbaya-yudi-sadewa|Purbaya]] says public confidence in the presidency recovered after falling in the middle of the year.

Purbaya also describes ambitious education ideas. These include integrated schools and *sekolah rakyat*, or people's schools. The interview mentions a hoped-for scale of thousands of schools.

## Connections in this wiki

- The Finance Ministry watches public confidence and social stability as part of its strategy for managing money and liquidity.
- MBG and *sekolah rakyat* are described as imperfect programs that can still help distribute the results of development.
- Prabowo provides political support for investment task forces, removing practical barriers, and increasing the capital available to LPDP scholarships.
