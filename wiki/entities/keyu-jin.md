# Keyu Jin

Economist at the London School of Economics; author of *The New China Playbook: Beyond Socialism and Capitalism*. Guest on [[entities/endgame-podcast|Endgame]] [[sources/009-endgame-239-keyu-jin|#239]] with [[entities/gita-wirjawan|Gita Wirjawan]].

## Formation (self-narrated)

- Left China at 14 for Horace Mann (NY) — rare then; only international student in her cohort
- Chinese exam discipline + US “question authority / open mind” (history teacher Mr. DeVito) as combined vantage
- Parents: Shakespearean scholar father; concert pianist mother (per Gita’s framing)
- Economics at Harvard for breadth (history, society, psychology, political economy) + precise tools
