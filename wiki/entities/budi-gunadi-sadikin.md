# Budi Gunadi Sadikin

Budi Gunadi Sadikin is an Indonesian cabinet minister responsible for health. He also speaks about public digital and AI infrastructure. In 2026, he joined [[sources/007-bravo-500-ai-panel|Bravo 500 / Endgame AI panel]] with [[entities/franky-widjaja|Franky Widjaja]] and [[entities/sai-prasad|Sai Prasad]]. [[entities/gita-wirjawan|Gita Wirjawan]] moderated the discussion.

## Why he appears here

Budi focuses on infrastructure limits and government policy. He uses Jensen Huang's five layers of AI: energy, computer chips, data centers, models, and applications.

He discusses an unsuccessful attempt to deregulate **power wheeling**, which would allow electricity to be transmitted across another company's network. He also covers access to TSMC's chip manufacturing, regulation based on broad principles, and the need to keep the technology sector competitive.

He says he directed LPDP scholarships toward the digital economy and the “three Bays.” He also warns that Indonesia can train people in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics but lose them to Singapore if they have no place at home to apply their skills.
