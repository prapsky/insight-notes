Indonesian cabinet minister (Health) and public-sector voice on digital/AI infrastructure. Panelist in [[sources/007-bravo-500-ai-panel|Bravo 500 / Endgame AI panel]] (2026) with [[entities/franky-widjaja|Franky Widjaja]] and [[entities/sai-prasad|Sai Prasad]], moderated by [[entities/gita-wirjawan|Gita Wirjawan]].

In the Bravo 500 panel he carries the **infrastructure-bottleneck** and **policy** lens: Jensen Huang’s five AI layers (energy → chips → DC → models → apps), failed **power-wheeling** deregulation push, TSMC access, principle-based regulation for competitive tech, steered LPDP toward digital economy / “three Bays,” and STEM-without-playground brain drain to Singapore.
