# Vannevar Bush

Vannevar Bush was an American engineer and science administrator. In 1945, he proposed the [[concepts/memex|Memex]]. It was an idea for a personal store of knowledge connected by associations.

## Why he appears here

[[sources/001-llm-wiki-pattern]] presents Bush as a conceptual ancestor of the [[concepts/llm-wiki|LLM Wiki]]. Both ideas involve privately choosing information and creating trails between documents. Bush did not solve the problem of maintaining such a system. Large language models may help with that work.
