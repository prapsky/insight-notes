# Franky O. Widjaja

Franky O. Widjaja is an Indonesian businessman. Event coverage places him around the chairman level at [[entities/sinarmas|Sinarmas]], especially in agribusiness, telecommunications, and technology. He was a panelist in the 2026 [[sources/007-bravo-500-ai-panel|Bravo 500 / Endgame AI panel]].

## Why he appears here

Franky takes a practical view of the application layer, where people use AI in real products and services. He says the group should participate in power generation, including coal, geothermal, and solar power, as well as data centers. The panel cites more than 40 megawatts of data-center capacity.

He argues that Indonesia should not try to build its own version of DeepSeek. Instead, it should [[concepts/copy-with-pride-application-layer|copy with pride]] by adopting useful technology and adapting it to local needs.

He says people are the only real asset. Companies should retain science and engineering talent with competitive pay and bring in mentors from abroad. The main challenges are the discomfort caused by organizational change and the need to choose solutions that are reliable and safe.
