# Michael Levitt

Professor of structural biology at Stanford Medical School (association also in computer science). Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2013 with Martin Karplus and Arieh Warshel for computational methods that made molecular modeling of chemical reactions / protein dynamics practicable.
