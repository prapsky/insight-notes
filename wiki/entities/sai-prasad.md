# Sai Prasad

Sai Prasad is presented as a Google Cloud executive or operator working in Southeast Asia. He provides an engineer's perspective in the 2026 [[sources/007-bravo-500-ai-panel|Bravo 500 / Endgame AI panel]]. In the same conversation, Gita uses names such as “Sai Prasad” when discussing how India's diaspora supports a [[concepts/brain-gain-and-circulation|brain linkage]] model.

## Why he appears here

Prasad explains [[concepts/physical-vs-cryptographic-sovereignty|physical vs cryptographic sovereignty]]. He also discusses the shortage of mid-level talent needed to turn AI prototypes into working products. He compares the energy needs of generative AI with those of AI agents. Other topics include distributed and edge computing, Singapore's government sandboxes for testing new ideas, and Southeast Asia's ability to keep several technology options open.

He describes AI as about 10% of the full technology stack. His engine-versus-airline comparison separates the underlying technology from the service built on top of it. These practical views connect to [[concepts/government-4-0-and-agile-regulation|Government 4.0]] and [[concepts/multipolar-balancing-and-asean|multipolar balancing]].
