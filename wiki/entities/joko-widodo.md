# Joko Widodo (Jokowi)

Former President of Indonesia. In [[sources/002-endgame-245-purbaya-sadewa|Endgame #245]], [[entities/purbaya-yudi-sadewa|Purbaya]] uses the Jokowi era as the **government-led / private-constrained** archetype: active state, weaker private room, single-digit credit and lower money growth, average growth near ~5%.

## Role in the dual-engine thesis

Counterpart to [[entities/susilo-bambang-yudhoyono|SBY]] in the “combine both engines” story toward ~6.5%. Also the period when Purbaya says he helped on debottlenecking (2016–19) and later moved to LPS.
