# Joko Widodo (Jokowi)

Joko Widodo, commonly called Jokowi, is a former President of Indonesia. In [[sources/002-endgame-245-purbaya-sadewa|Endgame #245]], [[entities/purbaya-yudi-sadewa|Purbaya]] uses the Jokowi era as an example of growth led by government while private business had less room.

Purbaya describes an active state, weaker conditions for private business, credit growth below 10%, lower money growth, and average economic growth near 5%.

## Role in the two-engine idea

The Jokowi era is the government-led counterpart to the private-business model associated with [[entities/susilo-bambang-yudhoyono|SBY]]. Purbaya argues that combining both engines could move growth toward 6.5%.

Purbaya also says that he helped remove investment barriers from 2016 to 2019 during this period. He later moved to LPS, Indonesia's deposit insurance agency.
