# Purbaya Yudi Sadewa

Indonesian economist and public official; **Menteri Keuangan** at the time of [[sources/002-endgame-245-purbaya-sadewa|Endgame #245]]. Guest of [[entities/gita-wirjawan|Gita Wirjawan]] on [[entities/endgame-podcast|Endgame]].

## Arc (self-narrated in interview)

- ITB electrical engineering → Schlumberger (field, high pay, extreme hours)
- Left industry for marriage conditions → PhD economics at Purdue (S1→S3 jump; brutal early months)
- Danareksa Research Institute (~2000–2014); informal/formal advising to [[entities/susilo-bambang-yudhoyono|SBY]] orbit, later [[entities/joko-widodo|Jokowi]] / Luhut orbit
- Directed to LPS during financial stress (~2020); nearly retired → appointed Finance Minister under [[entities/prabowo-subianto|Prabowo]]

## Policy signature (as presented)

- [[concepts/money-supply-vs-interest-rates|Watch M0 / money growth, not rates alone]]
- [[concepts/expectation-management|Expectation flip + immediate instruments]]
- Relocate idle government cash BI → banks; push double-digit money growth while watching inflation
- Dual growth engines (private + government) toward ~6.5%; [[concepts/fdi-climate|FDI]] for the step to ~8%
- [[concepts/investment-debottlenecking|Debottlenecking satgas]] + budget leverage; [[concepts/uncertainty-to-risk-translation|uncertainty → priced risk]]; [[concepts/stem-talent-pipeline|STEM / LPDP scale-up]]

## Intellectual anchors he cites

Milton Friedman (money growth / Depression framing); Bernanke on crises; “microeconomics of self-fulfilling prophecy” (Farmer); 1998 Indonesia as contradictory tight-rate / loose-money episode.
