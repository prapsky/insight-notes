# Purbaya Yudi Sadewa

Purbaya Yudi Sadewa is an Indonesian economist and public official. He was **Menteri Keuangan**, or Finance Minister, at the time of [[sources/002-endgame-245-purbaya-sadewa|Endgame #245]]. He was interviewed by [[entities/gita-wirjawan|Gita Wirjawan]] on [[entities/endgame-podcast|Endgame]].

## Career arc (as he tells it)

Purbaya studied electrical engineering at ITB. He then worked in the field for Schlumberger. The job paid well but required extremely long hours.

He left industry because of conditions connected to his marriage. He then entered an economics PhD program at Purdue without first completing a master's degree. The jump from undergraduate study to a doctorate made the first months very difficult.

From about 2000 to 2014, he worked at the Danareksa Research Institute. He advised people around [[entities/susilo-bambang-yudhoyono|SBY]], both informally and formally. He later worked around [[entities/joko-widodo|Jokowi]] and Luhut.

During financial stress around 2020, he was directed to lead LPS, Indonesia's deposit insurance agency. He nearly retired before being appointed Finance Minister under [[entities/prabowo-subianto|Prabowo]].

## Policy approach (as presented)

- Watch the growth of money in the system (including M0, a narrow money measure), not interest rates alone. See [[concepts/money-supply-vs-interest-rates]].
- Change public expectations, then quickly use concrete policy tools. See [[concepts/expectation-management]].
- Move idle government cash from Bank Indonesia into commercial banks. Aim for double-digit money growth while monitoring inflation.
- Use two growth engines: private business and government spending. He argues that these could produce about 6.5% growth. Better conditions for [[concepts/fdi-climate|foreign direct investment (FDI)]] would be needed to reach about 8%.
- Use a task force that removes barriers to investment, together with budget pressure. See [[concepts/investment-debottlenecking]]. Help investors turn vague uncertainty into risks they can estimate and price. See [[concepts/uncertainty-to-risk-translation]]. Expand STEM training and LPDP scholarships. See [[concepts/stem-talent-pipeline]].

## Thinkers and examples he cites

Purbaya cites Milton Friedman on money growth and the Great Depression. He refers to Bernanke's work on financial crises. He also mentions Farmer's “microeconomics of self-fulfilling prophecy.” He presents Indonesia in 1998 as a case with tight interest-rate policy but loose money growth.
